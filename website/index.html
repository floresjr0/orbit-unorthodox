Keep the work, the people, and the deadline in one orbit.

ORBIT is the Android app for student groups, studios, and small teams. A leader invites members with a code, assigns the work, and follows progress. Members update their own tasks, including when the connection drops.

Invitation codes

Each team has an unpredictable code. Leaders can expire it, cap uses, or require approval.

Shared tasks

Assign one task to several people, with priority, a checklist, and a deadline.

Review when it matters

Turn on verification and a completed task waits for the leader before it counts as done.

Offline, then sync

Status updates stay on the phone. A newer change on the server is kept when both sides edited the status.

How it works

01

Create a team

The creator becomes the leader and receives an invitation code.

02

Members join

They enter the code. If approval is on, they wait until the leader accepts them.

03

Assign and follow

Members move their own tasks. Leaders see overdue work, reviews, and activity.

For leaders

Membership, announcements, verification, reports, and a CSV export of the team’s workload.

For members

One account can sit on many teams. You change the tasks assigned to you.

Questions

Does a member see another team’s tasks?

Team access is checked on the server for every request.

What happens without a network?

Previously synced teams and tasks stay available. Status changes and comments wait in a queue and sync when the connection returns.

Where do I get the app?

From the download page, once an Android APK has been published. There is no browser edition of the app.
