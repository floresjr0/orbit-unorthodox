About

A shared place for the work a team actually owns.

ORBIT is built for groups that outgrow a chat thread: academic projects, student organizations, small studios, and professional workgroups. One account can belong to many teams. Leadership stays with the person who created the team, until they transfer it.

The Android app talks to an ORBIT server. The server keeps the record. The phone keeps a copy so a dropped connection does not erase a status update.

Unorthodox Technologies is the independent studio behind ORBIT. Founder Marte Flores Jr. and co-founders Justine Reyes, Melvin Ramos, and Clarenz Geronimo design and build the product.
