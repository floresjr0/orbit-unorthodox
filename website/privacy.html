Privacy

Privacy policy

ORBIT stores the account details you provide: name, email, password hash, and optional profile fields. Team names, tasks, comments, announcements, and activity history are stored so the people in that team can do the work.

Push notifications, when enabled, include a short title such as a task name. They do not include the full text of comments or task descriptions. Device tokens are stored only to deliver those notifications and are removed when they are no longer valid.

Platform administrators can see account status, reports, and platform audit events. They do not receive private team content through the administration tools.

You can delete your account from the profile screen. If you lead a team, transfer leadership first. Deletion replaces your name and email and signs out your devices.

Password reset messages are sent only when mail is configured. Until then, a local installation records the reset link in the server log.
